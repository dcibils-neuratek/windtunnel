Solver validation

Each case runs on a 96×44×44 lattice, discards the first 400 steps, then averages the momentum-exchange force over the next 300. Reference values are the full-scale textbook figures for the same shape. At this grid the effective Reynolds number is ~10³, where rounded and streamlined bodies genuinely have much higher Cd than their full-scale numbers — so what is being checked here is that the signs, the ordering and the Reynolds-appropriate magnitudes are right, not that the numbers match the right-hand column. Lift cells are green when the sign follows the angle of attack.
